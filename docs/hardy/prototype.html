Hardy the Hammer

Prototype · sample data

A working test of Forge's all-in-one assistant. The athletes, meals and lifts are invented sample data, and the screens are simplified stand-ins for Forge's real pages. Tap Hardy in the phone's corner: ask about any day, tell him what to change, or ask for a report. He takes you to the page, and nothing changes until you confirm.

Things I'd leave out (off by default)

Off because an assistant that interrupts gets turned off. On here, Hardy shows one tip per page beside his button.

Off because read-aloud was switched off in Forge on 2026-10-04 until people ask for it. On here, each answer gets a Read aloud button.

Off because Forge's rule is zero contact between athletes, to keep minors safe. On here, "tell Jordan nice lift" works for athletes too. Coach-to-athlete messages always work.

Guardrails

  • Pricing: Basic gets the finder; AI Coach unlocks advice, changes and reports, so Hardy can't give the $9.99 tier away.
  • No medical answers on any plan. "My knee hurts" gets "see a doctor or athletic trainer" and an injury-notes report to show them.
  • Hardy never changes a password, deletes an account or removes a coach. An email change waits for the link sent to the new address.
  • Patterns are observations, never cause and effect. Camera numbers carry the accuracy note.
  • For counsel before launch: Hardy reads a minor's whole history and sends parts of it to the AI provider.

Built into this test

  • Hammer in the corner, black to red; tap to open and close. Plain search tab with no AI.
  • Deep questions: heaviest lift, diet before it, weekly volume, time between PRs, the program block.
  • Changes with confirm cards: email, phone, height, body weight, nutrition targets, moving a planned workout.
  • Logging by talking: food, a meal photo, check-ins, "scan this" for the barcode scanner.
  • Schedule help, reminders, goals with real pace, meet mode with a countdown.
  • Hardy tab: 13 reports with PDF download and send to coach, weekly recap (opt-in), notes from athletes for coaches.
  • Recommendations for AI Coach and coaches: next-session weights from your check-ins, deload weeks, protein gaps.
  • Chats are not saved. Reports are, because you chose to save them. 50 questions a day.
  • With AI allowed here, Hardy answers through Claude using the same functions; without it, his built-in finder answers.
