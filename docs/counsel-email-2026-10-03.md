Subject: Forge Performance Systems: six questions before launch

Hi,

Forge has been submitted to Apple for review and we expect to open sign-ups in the next few
weeks. Six questions on the documents you reviewed, all wording or enforceability. None of
them changes the structure. We would like to fold every answer into one new version of the
affected documents so our users are asked to re-accept once, not six times.

1. Terms of Use, section 16 (Indemnification). Can this clause apply where the person
   indemnifying is a minor athlete or their parent or guardian? Several states limit or void
   an indemnity imposed on a minor. If it cannot, how should the section read for minors?

2. Institutional Service Agreement: the school's duty to obtain guardian consent. Is that
   allocation enforceable as written, and does Forge need anything on its side beyond the
   clause (a confirmation from the school, a record we keep) to rely on it?

3. Institutional Service Agreement: the indemnity for a consent failure. Please confirm the
   clause does what we need it to, and whether it interacts with question 1, since the
   consumer Terms of Use carries its own minors issue.

4. Verifiable parental consent for athletes under 13. Today a parent claims a linked account
   from an emailed link, and the account does nothing until they do. That is email-only
   verification. Is that sufficient for COPPA, and if not, which second step do you want: a
   follow-up confirmation, a small card transaction once billing is on, or not accepting
   under-13s until it is settled?

5. Apple Health data reaching our AI provider. The app pre-fills a daily check-in from Apple
   Health (sleep, resting heart rate, HRV, VO2 max, respiratory rate, weight, session heart
   rate), and the AI coach reads that check-in when it adjusts training, so a Health-derived
   value can appear inside a prompt sent to Anthropic. The Privacy Policy discloses this, and
   the Health switch in the app says a synced value can reach the AI provider for coaching,
   never sold, never for advertising. Is a coaching recommendation a "fitness purpose" under
   Apple's HealthKit terms, is the switch sufficient permission, and is the sentence on the
   switch enough?

6. The launch email list. The website now takes an email address from anyone who wants to
   hear when sign-ups open, with an unsubscribe link in every mailing. It is addresses only,
   not linked to any account. The Privacy Policy describes account holders' data and does not
   mention this list. Does it need a sentence (what is collected, that it is used only for
   Forge's own news and offers, how to unsubscribe), and if so where?

For each, a yes/no plus any replacement wording is ideal. We register each change as a new
version with its date, so wording you send can go in verbatim.

Thanks,
Scott Morrow
Forge Performance Systems LLC
