# The blind shock: specification, written BEFORE the run

**Status: pre-registration. Nothing has been run. No result exists.**

This file exists so that the latent-shock test cannot score its own ruler. Every number below is
declared now, in writing, in a commit, before the harness is touched. If a figure in here changes
after a run, that is a new specification and it says so, with the old one left in place.

The precedent is this repo's own: `FITTED_OVERRIDE_EVIDENCE` in `shared/camera-tunables-by-lift.ts`,
and the 2026-10-09 finding that `plateBoxToExpectedRatio` divided by the very quantity it was
scoring, so it returned 1.000 on a plate that was 60% wrong. **A diagnostic that is free to be
tuned after seeing the answer measures nothing.**

---

## 0 · What this is testing

The Anvil's central claim, as written: *Forge predicts today's reading from training load and sleep
before the athlete taps anything; the check either agrees or disagrees, and **the disagreement is
the useful part**.*

That is a hypothesis. It has never been tested, and this is the test.

### Three things found while reading the harness, which change the shape of the test

1. **The implemented slow channel regresses out LOAD ONLY.** `compare.py :: chronic()` fits
   `drift ~ load` across athletes and takes the residual. Sleep is nowhere in it.
2. **Sleep is generated by all three world models and never emitted.** `sim125b.py`, `mlb2.py` and
   `nba.py` each compute `slp` per athlete-day and feed it into a sleep-debt pool, but the row
   written to JSON carries `load`, `acute`, `chronic`, `wear`, `v` — and no sleep. No detector
   could read it even if one wanted to.
3. **Therefore the predict-then-check claim has never been built.** Testing it means building the
   predictor, which is exactly the circularity this file exists to prevent. So the predictor's
   form is declared in §4 below, before any shock is run, and it is not permitted to change.

## 1 · The shock, in the world

A **non-training stressor**: an exam period, a family disruption, an acute personal-life shock.
Something that degrades an athlete without appearing in a training log.

Realism bound, stated because it limits the conclusion: real academic stress degrades sleep. A
shock that touches nothing a coach can see is the easy case and is not the interesting one. Both
are run, as separate arms.

## 2 · The shock, in the harness

Units are the harness's own. The observable `v` is a multiplicative drive value around each
athlete's `drive0`; per-capture noise is `NOISE = 0.0115` of it; one unit of modelled decline is
`FAT = 0.0022`.

| property | declared value |
|---|---|
| affected share of roster | **15%**, drawn uniformly, independent of position, tier and decline kind |
| temporal profile | linear ramp **3 days** → plateau **12 days** → exponential decay **τ = 5 days** |
| onset | uniform over the middle 60% of each athlete's own season span, drawn independently |
| magnitude at plateau | swept over **{0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0} × NOISE**, i.e. {0.00575, 0.0115, 0.01725, 0.0230} subtracted from `v` |
| applied to | `v` **directly**, never through the acute / chronic / wear pools |

**The last row is the whole point.** Routing the shock through the fatigue pools would make it
something the detector was built to see. It is applied to the observable through a pathway the
model does not contain, because that is what "latent" means.

The magnitude **sweep** is declared rather than a single value so that no one magnitude can be
chosen after the fact for being the one that works.

## 3 · Three arms

| arm | shock on `v` | effect on sleep | effect on load | purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A · latent** | yes | none | none | can the residual see anything at all |
| **B · partially visible** | yes | **−0.6 SD** of that athlete's own sleep distribution, over the same window | none | **does the residual add anything beyond sleep** |
| **C · null** | none | none | none | the false-positive floor, same seeds |

**Arm B is the test that decides it.** Arm A asks whether the thing can detect. Arm B asks for
**incremental validity** — whether Converge beats simply looking at sleep. A product whose
headline is "the disagreement" has to beat the agreement.

Arm C is not optional. Every rate below is reported against it, and a detection rate quoted
without its floor is meaningless.

## 4 · The four detectors, declared now

1. **Fast channel** — unchanged. Own rolling baseline, last 20 captures, flag at z ≥ 2.0.
   `compare.py :: acute()`, untouched.
2. **Slow channel** — unchanged. Fixed anchor, load regressed out, threshold pinned to the clean
   group's 90th centile. `compare.py :: chronic()`, untouched.
3. **Sleep-only** — the benchmark arm B must beat. Mean sleep over the window against the
   athlete's own anchor-window mean, standardised the same way the slow channel standardises its
   residual (median / MAD ÷ 0.6745), threshold pinned to the clean group's 90th centile.
4. **Converge** — the hypothesis. Identical to the slow channel except the regression carries
   **two** predictors, load and sleep, both as window-mean minus anchor-mean, and the flag is on
   the residual. Ordinary least squares, two terms, no interaction, no regularisation.

Detector 4 is detector 2 **plus one column**. That is deliberate: a difference between them is
attributable to the sleep term and nothing else.

### What must change in the harness, and nothing else

- `sim125b.py`, `mlb2.py`, `nba.py`: add `"slp": round(slp, 2)` to the emitted row. Regenerate.
- A new file for the shock injection and the four detectors. **The three world generators are not
  otherwise edited**, and the existing `compare.py` is not edited at all — it is imported.

## 5 · What counts as a result, declared before the run

- Every rate is reported **with its spread across seeds**, never as a point estimate. This repo has
  had the same figure wrong by 2× and by 5× from small samples on three separate occasions.
- Detection is measured **at a false-positive rate of 10%**, the rate already fixed by the design,
  with arm C confirming the floor is actually 10% and not something else.

**Arm A — the floor test.** At the magnitude where the fast channel detects, Converge must detect
above arm C's floor by more than the two spreads combined. If Converge cannot see a fully latent
shock, the hypothesis is dead on arrival and nothing in arm B matters.

**Arm B — the real test.** Converge's detection must exceed the **sleep-only** detector's by more
than the two spreads combined, at the same false-positive rate.

**Falsification, stated in advance so it cannot be renegotiated:** if arm B shows Converge ≈
sleep-only, the disagreement adds nothing over looking at sleep, and **that is the finding.** It is
written up as a negative result and the shock is not re-tuned until it passes. The honest outcome
of a test you can fail is sometimes failing it.

**Non-results, named so they are not reported as results:**
- Converge beating the load-only slow channel in arm B is not a finding. Sleep moved by
  construction in that arm; a detector reading sleep ought to beat one that cannot.
- Any single magnitude in the sweep working while its neighbours do not is noise, not a threshold.

## 6 · What this test cannot say

- It is a simulation. A shock that is detectable here is detectable **in a world whose rules I
  wrote**. It can falsify the hypothesis; it cannot confirm it.
- −0.6 SD on sleep is a guess at how hard a real academic stressor hits sleep. It is declared, not
  measured, and the conclusion is conditional on it. A sensitivity pass over {0.3, 0.6, 1.0} is run
  and reported alongside.
- Nothing here measures compliance, which the earlier runs put at ~37% of detection. A shocked
  athlete who stops opening the app is a separate and probably larger problem.
